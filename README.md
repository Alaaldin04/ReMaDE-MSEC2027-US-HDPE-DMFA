# ReMaDE-MSEC2027-US-HDPE-DMFA

## Overview

This repository hosts the Supplementary Information (SI) and supporting materials for the MSEC 2027 conference paper:

**Evaluating U.S. HDPE Circularity Through Quality-Resolved Dynamic Material Flow Analysis**

The study develops a dynamic material flow analysis (DMFA) framework to evaluate the circularity of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) in the United States from 2025 to 2060.

The framework integrates material flows, product lifespans, end-of-use scrap generation, collection, sorting, and recycling with application-specific melt flow index (MFI) compatibility constraints.

The study investigates how collection rates, sorting resolution, recycling technologies, and demand growth affect recycled content, material retention, economic cost, and primary energy consumption.

## Supplementary Information

The Supplementary Information provides additional details supporting the methodology and results presented in the paper, including:

- Model assumptions, system boundaries, and input parameters.
- Historical demand, product lifespan, and application-grade distributions.
- MFI mixing rules and recycled-resin compatibility constraints.
- Sorting and recycling scenario definitions.
- Mathematical formulation of the optimization framework.
- Additional tables, figures, and supporting results.
